    Yes — we offer free shipping on all orders above ₹2000 across India. Orders are dispatched fresh and shipped in eco-friendly packaging.

    Buransh juice is made from the rare rhododendron flower that blooms in the Himalayas. It is floral and tangy, naturally rich in antioxidants, and traditionally enjoyed as a refreshing, heart-friendly summer drink.
